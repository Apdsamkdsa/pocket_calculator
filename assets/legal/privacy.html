Privacy Policy

Pocket Calculator · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how Pocket Calculator (the “App”) handles information. The App is a loan calculator for personal, educational use.

1. Information we process

Loan amount, interest rate, and term that you enter stay on your device. We do not create an account and we do not sell personal data.

The device may process technical data needed to run the App (for example, crash diagnostics provided by the operating system).

2. What we do not collect

  • Name, email, or payment card numbers
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone audio

3. On-device storage

A flag that you finished onboarding is stored locally with the operating system’s preferences API. Clearing the App’s data resets that flag.

4. Calculations

Monthly payment, interest, and amortization are computed on the device. Results are estimates, not a credit offer or financial advice.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it if you are under the age required in your region.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email listed on the App Store or Google Play listing for Pocket Calculator.
